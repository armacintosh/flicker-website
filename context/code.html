ABOUT THE GAME

The deep ocean is beautiful. It also wants you dead.

Flicker is a vertical shoot 'em up where you pilot an adorable snailfish through 5 levels of increasingly dangerous deep-sea territory. Dodge bullet storms, discover power-ups, and face off against some of the ocean's most terrifying real-world creatures — the ones nature already made into nightmares.

Beautiful hand-crafted bioluminescent pixel art, original music for every level, and bosses that are genuinely unsettling. Available on Steam, iOS, and Android.
