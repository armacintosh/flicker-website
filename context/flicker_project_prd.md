# Flicker — Project Brief & Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** Flicker  
**Developer:** Butterware Studios  
**Platform:** Steam, iOS, Android  
**Status:** In-Development (v2 Website Phase)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Flicker is a high-fidelity, vertical shoot 'em up (shmup) that explores the "cute but creepy" duality of the deep ocean. Players pilot a tiny, bioluminescent snailfish through five levels of escalating difficulty, culminating in encounters with enormous, terrifying bosses based on real-world deep-sea creatures.

The project goal for this phase is the launch of a high-conversion marketing website aimed at driving Steam wishlists and building brand awareness through TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Voice
*   **Cute but Creepy:** The contrast between the adorable protagonist and the terrifying, massive scale of the bosses.
*   **Real Monsters:** Grounding the fantasy in real-world marine biology (Yeti Crab, Pelican Eel, Blackseadevil).
*   **Bioluminescent Aesthetic:** High-contrast visuals using deep navies, vibrant teals, and signature pinks.
*   **Indie Grit:** Honest, dry, and challenging. A 5-level focused experience that prioritizes quality over bloat.

---

## 3. Key Features
*   **Bullet-Hell Gameplay:** 5 handcrafted levels with escalating bullet patterns and power-up systems.
*   **Boss-Centric Design:** Every level anchors on a biologically inspired boss with unique mechanics.
*   **Pixel Art Excellence:** 2D pixel art rendered with pixel-perfect scaling, emphasizing atmospheric lighting.
*   **Adaptive Soundtrack:** Original score for every level and boss encounter to enhance immersion.

## 5. Website Requirements (v2)
### 5.1 Conversion Goals
1.  **Primary:** Steam Wishlist (Main CTA).
2.  **Secondary:** YouTube Trailer views (Lightbox modal).
3.  **Tertiary:** Social follows (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).

### 5.2 Section Architecture
*   **Hero:** Dramatic "Small vs. Big" visual with the Snailfish and Boss eyes.
*   **About:** Clear 50/50 split of gameplay GIF and "Death in the Deep" narrative.
*   **Features:** Three-card layout (Fast & Frantic, Real Monsters, Original Soundtrack).
*   **Media Gallery:** 5-tile horizontal showcase of animations and environments.
*   **Meet the Bosses:** Animated cards featuring boss sprites, scientific names, and descriptions.
*   **Footer:** Global navigation, social links, and Butterware branding.

---

## 6. Technical Specifications
*   **Framework:** HTML5/Tailwind CSS with Vanilla JS for animations.
*   **Motion:** `IntersectionObserver` for staggered fade-up entrances; CSS pulses for bioluminescence.
*   **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first design with sticky navigation and tap-friendly targets (44px min).
*   **Asset Rendering:** `image-rendering: pixelated` for all pixel art assets to ensure sharpness.

---

## 7. Success Metrics
*   **Steam Wishlist Conversion Rate:** Targeting 5% of unique site visitors.
*   **Trailer Engagement:** 30%+ click-through rate on the "Watch Trailer" CTA.
*   **Social Growth:** Steady follower increase across `@flickergame` handles.
